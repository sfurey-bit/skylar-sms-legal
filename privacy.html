Effective date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sean Furey (“I,” “me,” or “Sean”) collects and uses information in connection with SMS messages sent by my personal AI assistant Skylar on my behalf.

Who this covers

People who have consented to receive text messages from Sean Furey / Skylar about personal scheduling and logistics (for example dinner plans, appointment reminders, and availability checks).

Information we process

  • Phone numbers you provide or that I already have for contacting you

How we use information

Only to send and manage consented SMS for personal assistant / scheduling purposes, to honor opt-out requests, and to meet carrier and legal compliance requirements (including A2P 10DLC).

Service providers

SMS is delivered using Twilio as a processor. Twilio processes phone numbers and message data to transmit texts. I do not sell your personal information.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help. You may also email sfurey@hyperspeedtechnologies.com.

Retention

Message and consent records are kept only as long as needed for messaging, troubleshooting, and compliance, then deleted or minimized.
